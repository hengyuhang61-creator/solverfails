# What a passing check leaves open

We evaluate one concept. Pair a specific accepted claim with evidence that could independently contradict it. A false acceptance requires a passing baseline, an identified target and a negative reference verdict for that target. Keep a missing verdict as UNKNOWN. Numerical consistency and evidence attribution have different units, failure mechanisms and sources of truth. Do not pool their scores.

## Frozen protocol

Written before running the evaluation matrix. Development sources were already inspected, including both model excerpts. Holdouts below are designed stress families reserved from rule selection. They are reproducible, unblinded and share code with the development set. Neither model output is a blind holdout.

* Grid target — absolute error of a reported continuum scalar at most 1e-6. The real Poiseuille integration test accepts an observed order strictly between 1.5 and 2.5; it makes no assertion about that scalar. Apply this actual predicate to manufactured inputs as a simulated gate. Compare the coarse-oriented expression and fine-oriented Richardson expression on the same three values. Given a separate fourth observation, use the observed ratio of successive differences from the first three levels to estimate the limit from levels three and four. Accept if the proposed limit agrees within tolerance. Abstain outside decreasing, monotone differences on equal-ratio grids, when data are missing, or if the ratio is numerically indistinguishable from one. This local geometric assumption can fail. Reference labels come from the generating function at zero, independent of the fourth observation. The actual upstream fixture has a separately specified analytic velocity profile and a zero limiting interpolation error. Repeat frozen grid decisions at 1e-7 and 1e-5, without selecting a threshold from the outcomes.
* Grid development — single powers 1 and 2. Hold out fractional powers 1.5 and 2.5 and a two-term error law. Cross absolute limits 0 and 1, signs and amplitudes −1, 1, 1e-5, refinement factors 2 and 3, starting spacings 1 and 0.1. Compare both estimators. Include equality, missing fourth level and a real-data question without an analytic truth. These are correlated variants.
* Survey target — does the cited evidence entail the stated numerical population bound? Preserve the distinction between support and actual population prevalence. Manually identify the source, descriptive percentage, target population and comparison; an unparsed or modal statement may require abstention. The baseline recorded for the historical excerpts is the original complete hearing validator. Synthetic variants simulate citation presence only. A revised check requires a valid reference and checks the range of population rates consistent with the survey. Known sample and population counts give sharp lower and upper bounds with no assumption about nonrespondents. With only a reported fraction, keep that fraction for respondents. For an unspecified larger population there is no nontrivial guaranteed closed interval. Published percentages are replayed at their stated precision. The real refutation uses explicit possible populations, independent of any estimate of the clinic population.
* Survey controls — vary sample size, affected count, nonresponse and exact or interval claims. Label finite controls by enumerating individual missing binary responses. Use n=4 for development; reserve n=5 and n=7 for evaluation, including census and one nonrespondent. Include inclusive endpoint, foreign ID, ambiguous target and absent truth. A wrong citation fails the revised check even if a numerical value happens to be true.
* Report raw rows and errors by family, provenance and split. A rule abstention is separate from rejection. Retain both truth-unknown cases and abstentions on known labels. Scores count candidate decisions, not studies or independent incidents. Historical software behavior on manufactured data, historical generated text on an authored scenario and synthetic variants are separate strata. No CFD validation or model leaderboard follows from these tests.
