How the check works

The estimate averages 32 equally spaced values on [0,1). A second set of 32 values checks it. Agreement within 0.02 gives a green signal. Each sampling rule gets exactly 64 evaluations. The analytical integral grades your choice after the reveal. These are constructed waveforms.
