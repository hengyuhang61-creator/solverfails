What happened

Both Mach 5 fields used the same 37,275-cell grid and adiabatic SST-2003m setup with the Wilcox correction. The 3,200-iteration state continued from the 1,700-iteration restart. The bottom-wall skin-friction zero moved from x = 322.72 to 315.52 mm. Reattachment moved from 348.51 to 349.55 mm.

What the checks establish

The boundary balance measures net mass, and the skin-friction crossings locate a local flow feature. In these two saved fields the mass test passed and both crossings exceeded the 1 mm stability tolerance. This failure applies to the selected iteration window and closure. The eventual settled location and physical accuracy remain open.
